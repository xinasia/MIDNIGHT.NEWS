THE CITY HAS A VOICE

TIO TOKYO · @tio__tokyo

Every city has countless stories waiting to be heard. Sometimes, all it takes is a simple question and a spontaneous conversation to discover something interesting about the people around us.

Tio Tokyo, known by the social media username @tio__tokyo, brings people and their perspectives into the spotlight through street interviews. With engaging conversations and spontaneous interactions, the content captures authentic reactions, interesting opinions and the unique personalities of everyday people.
