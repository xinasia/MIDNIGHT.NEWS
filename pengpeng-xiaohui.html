THE STORY

A simple conversation can reveal fascinating stories, unexpected opinions and different perspectives on life. Street interviews are more than just asking questions; they are about connecting with people, discovering their thoughts and capturing genuine human moments.

Pengpeng Xiaohui, known on social media as @pengpengxiaohui, is a street interviewer whose content brings everyday people and their stories into the spotlight. Through spontaneous conversations and interesting questions, she creates engaging moments that give viewers a glimpse into different personalities, opinions and experiences.

“Every stranger has a story, and every conversation is an opportunity to discover something new.”
